We want you to be happy with your order. This policy explains what to do if something goes wrong.

## Change of mind

We don't offer returns, refunds or exchanges if you simply change your mind, for example if you chose the wrong size or colour, or no longer want the item. Please check the product description, sizes and photos before you order, and email us if you have any questions first.

This does not affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law or the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act (see below).

## Faulty, damaged, wrong or not-as-described items

You are always covered if your item:

- arrives damaged or broken,
- is faulty or stops working when it shouldn't,
- is not what you ordered (wrong item, colour or size), or
- is significantly different from its description or photos.

**Australian customers:** our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

**New Zealand customers:** you have rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, including that goods are of acceptable quality, fit for purpose and match their description. If they don't, you are entitled to a remedy such as a repair, replacement or refund, depending on the problem.

### How to report a problem

1. Email us at **qualicartonlineshop@gmail.com** with your order number.
2. Tell us what's wrong and include clear photos (and a short video if the item doesn't work). For items damaged in transit, please also include a photo of the outer packaging.
3. We'll reply within 1 business day with the next steps.

Please contact us as soon as you can after delivery, ideally within 7 days for items damaged in transit, so we can follow it up with the carrier quickly. This request does not limit your rights under the law.

In many cases we can fix the problem without you sending anything back. If we do need the item returned, we will tell you where to send it and **we will cover the reasonable cost of return postage** for faulty, damaged, wrong or not-as-described items.

## Orders that don't arrive

If your tracking shows no movement for an unusually long time, or your order hasn't arrived after the estimated delivery window shown at checkout, email us. We will investigate with the carrier and, if the parcel is lost, send a replacement or give you a full refund.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order for a full refund before it has been dispatched. Orders are usually dispatched within 2 business days, so please email us as soon as possible. Once an order has been dispatched it can't be cancelled, but you are still covered for faulty, damaged, wrong or lost items as described above.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method. We aim to process refunds within 3 business days of approval. Your bank or payment provider may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show the refund in your account.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, a return or a refund: email **qualicartonlineshop@gmail.com**. We reply within 1 business day.
