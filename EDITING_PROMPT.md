# Qualicart — DSers Product Editing Master Prompt

Saved from the owner's instructions on 2026-11-15. Use this for every DSers Import List editing session.

Continue editing products in my Qualicart DSers Import List, starting from the product I specify and working toward the newest products.
Follow these instructions for every product. Complete and verify each product before moving to the next.

## 1. Product Information

Open the product editor in DSers and update:

- **Title:** Create a unique, factual, SEO-friendly title using Australian English. Keep it under 10 words and 80 characters where practical.
- **Collection:** Select the most appropriate existing Shopify collection. Do not create new collections.
- **Product Type:** Enter a specific and relevant product type.
- **Tags:** Add relevant SEO and product tags separately.
- **Vendor/Brand:** Do not change the vendor or brand field.
- **URL Handle:** Create a unique, SEO-friendly, lowercase, hyphen-separated handle. Preserve an existing correct handle when unnecessary changes could disrupt a listing.

Avoid unsupported marketing claims or invented specifications.

## 2. Product Description Format

Delete the original AliExpress supplier description and replace it with a professional, customer-friendly description.
Use the Specifications description editor and apply actual H2/H3 heading formatting, not just visually bold text.

Follow this structure:

- **H2: Product Description** — Write an engaging introductory paragraph explaining the product, its purpose, and its main benefits.
- **H3: Key Features** — Use bullet points with bold feature labels followed by concise explanations.
- **H3: Specifications** — Include only verified product information, such as:
  - Materials
  - Dimensions
  - Colours
  - Power requirements
  - Connectivity
  - Compatibility
  - Other relevant technical specifications
- **H3: Package Includes** — List the actual items included in the selected product variant.
- **H3: Why It's a Great Choice** — Explain the practical advantages, convenience, and suitable applications of the product.

Important:

- The first heading must be Product Description, not the product title.
- The final section must be Why It's a Great Choice, not Before You Buy.
- Do not invent technical specifications.
- Do not include unsupported delivery promises.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition and exaggerated claims.
- Use professional Australian English.
- Remove outdated supplier text and irrelevant information.

## 3. Inventory Verification

Check every variant against its actual supplier inventory.

- 0–4 units: Delete the variant.
- 5 or more units: Keep the variant.
- If no eligible variants remain, delete the product from the DSers Import List.
- Do not assume unavailable stock information means stock is sufficient.

Save the inventory changes before proceeding.

## 4. Variant Splitting

If a product has multiple eligible variants:

1. Select Split Products.
2. Choose Custom Split.
3. Select all remaining eligible variants.
4. Save.
5. Edit every resulting product individually.

Each resulting product must have its own:

- Unique SEO title
- Appropriate collection and type
- Relevant tags
- Unique URL handle
- Accurate description
- Correct variant-specific specifications
- Correct package contents

After verifying the resulting listings, delete the original combined listing if it is no longer required.
If only one eligible variant remains, keep it as a single product without unnecessary splitting.

## 5. Australia and New Zealand Shipping

Check shipping availability and estimated delivery times for both Australia and New Zealand.

Requirements:

- The supplier must ship to Australia.
- The supplier must also ship to New Zealand.
- Acceptable delivery time: 1–15 days.
- Check actual supplier shipping options rather than assuming delivery availability.
- Delete products that cannot meet the requirements.
- Avoid products with unsupported plug types or unsuitable electrical configurations for Australia.
- Return the shipping destination to Australia after checking New Zealand.

Do not invent shipping costs, transit times, or delivery availability.

## 6. Product Profitability

Where reliable supplier and marketplace pricing is accessible:

- Check the product cost plus shipping to Australia.
- Compare against relevant products on Kogan, Amazon Australia, and eBay Australia.
- Assess whether an estimated gross profit of ₱1,000–₱10,000 is achievable.
- Flag products that fail this target.
- Do not invent competitor prices or profit estimates.
- Do not change selling prices unless separately instructed.

Marketplace fees and other selling costs must be considered before treating any amount as net profit.

## 7. Save and Verify

After editing each product:

1. Save all changes.
2. Reopen the product.
3. Confirm the title, description, collection, type, tags, and URL.
4. Confirm remaining variants and stock.
5. Confirm the shipping checks have been completed.
6. Verify the edited information has persisted.
7. Proceed to the next product.

## 8. Deletion Rules

Delete from the DSers Import List when:

- No variant has at least 5 units in stock.
- Shipping to Australia is unavailable.
- Shipping to New Zealand is unavailable.
- Available delivery times do not meet the 1–15-day requirement.
- The original combined listing is redundant after successful variant splitting.

Do not delete active Shopify products under this Import List editing workflow without separate authorisation.

## 9. Restrictions

- Work inside the DSers Import List only.
- Do not push or publish products to Shopify.
- Do not place orders or make payments.
- Do not enter passwords.
- Do not change vendor or brand fields.
- Do not create unsupported technical specifications.
- Do not modify unrelated active Shopify listings.
- Do not delete eligible variants unnecessarily.
- Do not claim a product is complete until its changes have been saved and verified.

## 10. Progress Reporting

Maintain an accurate record of:

- Products successfully edited
- Variants deleted for insufficient stock
- Products successfully split
- Products deleted for failing shipping requirements
- Products requiring manual review
- Products that could not be verified

Clearly report anything skipped, blocked, or incomplete.
